# G2 — volatility (`volWindow`, `volStep`, `volPeriodsPerYear`, `sigmaRefBps`, `sigmaMultMaxBps`)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g2_volatility.py` (WP-7a). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.2](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g2_volatility.py`.

## What the parameters control

SIGMA-1 (state.cpp:1209-1220) samples **pFast** every `volStep` blocks over `volWindow` blocks,
annualises the sum of squared log returns with `volPeriodsPerYear` (= BLOCKS_PER_YEAR / volStep,
K13) and sets the multiplier `clamp(σ̂ / sigmaRefBps, 1×, sigmaMultMaxBps)`. MINT-5 multiplies the
class base ratio by it. One undefined (or pre-start) pFast sample pins the multiplier at the cap
(K12, fact 1.5-3).

## Method

σ̂ is always measured on **simulated pFast**, not on the true price (D-WP3-6): true paths → WP-3
oracle tag stream (honest pools, independent pool outages; the same oracle model as G1) → PRICE-1
median at the candidate's `pFastWindow` → `sim.sigma`. Ensembles (P paths each, quick 8; horizon
max(30 days, budget block horizon); statistics skip the first 8,064 + 288 blocks):

| Ensemble | Synthetic | With real data |
|---|---|---|
| `realised` | the four YEC-like presets (GBM, Merton, GARCH-t, regime switch), equal paths, centred | demeaned stationary block bootstrap of the real returns |
| `calm` | GBM at the regime-switch preset's calm σ (0.80) | GBM at the calm σ of a regime-switch fit |
| `turbulent` | Merton jump-diffusion with the preset jump law (12/yr, N(−2 %, 20 %)), diffusion set so total vol = the turbulent σ (2.00) | the same at the fitted turbulent σ |
| `shift` | calm for 10 days, then turbulent | same |
| `feed-outage-6h` | the scenario (all feeds down 6 h at day 10) | bootstrap base |

Candidates share these realisations (common random numbers); σ̂ itself is cheap.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `sigma_hat_p{5,50,95,99}_{ensemble}` | percentiles of σ̂ (bps, unclamped: `sigma.sigma_hat_bps`) over defined blocks × paths |
| `sigma_hat_cv_calm` (**primary**) | coefficient of variation of σ̂ in the stationary calm ensemble (estimator noise) |
| `mult_p{50,95,99}_{ensemble}` | the clamped multiplier (exact kernel) at the candidate's reference and cap |
| `cap_{undefined,vol}_h_per_year_{ensemble}` | hours/yr at the cap because a sample was undefined (K12) vs because σ̂ is high |
| `responsiveness_blocks` (`_p90`) | median (p90) over paths of blocks after the calm → turbulent shift until σ̂ first covers 90 % of the way from its 2-day pre-shift median to its median over the last quarter of the series |
| `k12_trap_h_per_outage`, `k12_trap_after_feed_blocks` | blocks with an undefined sample from the outage start (hours) / after the feeds return |
| `m14_ratio` | median σ̂ (realised) / sigmaRefBps — the unclamped median multiplier |
| `true_vol_bps_*` | realised volatility of the true price (context for D-WP3-6) |

## Decision rules (applied in this order)

1. **volWindow / volStep** — minimise `sigma_hat_cv_calm` subject to `responsiveness_blocks ≤
   max_sigma_lag_blocks` (4,032) and `k12_trap_after_feed_blocks ≤ max_sigma_lag_blocks` (the
   multiplier must also recover from a feed outage within the lag budget; D-WP7a-5), over the rows
   at the current reference and cap, through `decide_with_materiality` (20 %).
2. **sigmaRefBps** — rule value = median σ̂ of the realised ensemble at the chosen window/step,
   rounded **down** to `sigma_ref_round_bps` (500) so the median multiplier is ≥ 1×. KEEP while
   the current ratio σ̂₅₀/sigmaRef lies in `sigma_accept_band` [1.0, 1.5] and the rule value is
   within materiality of it; otherwise CHANGE to the rule value (D-WP7a-6).
3. **sigmaMultMaxBps** — rule value = smallest multiple of 2,500 ≥ the `sigma_mult_cap_pctl` (p99)
   turbulent multiplier at the recommended reference (σ̂₉₉,turb / ref, unclamped). KEEP while the
   current cap covers it and is within materiality above the rule value; CHANGE if it does not cover,
   or exceeds it by more than materiality (a cap above need only costs collateral in K12 traps);
   BLOCKED if the need exceeds the 50,000 bound.
4. **volPeriodsPerYear** — derived (K13).

All verdicts pass through `final_verdict` (synthetic → PROVISIONAL).

## Search space and budgets

| Budget | Candidates | Runtime (4 cores) |
|---|---|---|
| quick | volWindow {1,152, 2,016, 2,880, 4,032, 5,760} × volStep {24, 48, 96, 144} (divisible pairs), plus sigmaRef {6,000 … 15,000} and cap {20,000 … 40,000} one-at-a-time sweeps: 27 sets | ≈ 10 s |
| standard / deep | volWindow on the 144-block lattice thinned to `grid_points`, volStep {12, 24, 48, 96, 144, 288}, refs and caps thinned to `grid_points` | minutes |

The reference and cap sweeps are evidence and sensitivity (OAT); their values come from the rules.

## Assumptions

- The turbulent regime carries jumps (Merton): this is what drives the p99 multiplier, and with it
  the cap rule. Without jumps (plain GBM at σ 2.0; measured, 16 paths × 30 days, quick seed) the p99
  turbulent σ̂ is ≈ 19,700 bps — 2.8× a 7,000 reference, inside the shipped 3× cap — against
  ≈ 29,400 bps (4.2×) with jumps. The cap recommendation therefore hinges on whether YEC's
  turbulent spells carry 20 %-scale jumps; real data decides it.
- Pool-outage model as in G1 (K12 traps in the realised ensemble come from it).

## How real data changes the answer

`sigmaRefBps` is the parameter most directly set by data: the rule is the median pFast-based σ̂ of
the real price (bootstrapped), and the calm/turbulent volatilities come from a regime-switch fit to
it. Expect the reference to track real YEC volatility × the pFast smoothing factor (≈ 0.6–0.7 on
synthetic paths). Window/step choices depend little on the level of volatility (the CV is
scale-free) but do on its clustering.
