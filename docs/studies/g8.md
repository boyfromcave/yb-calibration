# G8 — price attestation (every v3 attest field of group G8)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g8_attestation.py`, ports in `src/ybcal/studies/_g8_ports.py` (WP-7c).
Plan: [PLAN.md §5.8](../PLAN.md); proposal `yellowback-price-attestation.md` §7, §10, §12, §16.
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g8_attestation.py`, `tests/studies/test_g8_ports.py`.

## Structure

The study is a set of **families**, one decision rule each, sharing one candidate table (one-at-a-time
sweeps around the current set). Kinds: **rule** (a ported closed-form target; KEEP when the current
value is within `materiality` of it), **optimize** (a primary metric with policy constraints through
`decide_with_materiality`), **verify** (KEEP unless a constraint fails; then the nearest value that
passes — used where the policy gives a bound but no cost to trade against).

## The ported measurements (proposal §16)

`_g8_ports.py` carries `spreads.py analyze` and `pinrate.py analyze` from ycash6 `7702d22` with the
upstream names and arithmetic (only the network halves stay upstream). `test_g8_ports.py` copies the
upstream scripts out of the clone with `git show` into a temp dir, imports them and compares outputs
on the same CSVs (random spreads logs with gaps, missing cells and a duplicate; random hourly
histories at four window/delta settings), plus the upstream unit cases as hand-computed values.

| Param | Rule |
|---|---|
| `divergeBpsAttest` | `diverge_spread_multiplier` (3) × worst source pair's nearest-rank p95 of `|a−b|·10⁴/min(a,b)`, rounded **up** to 100 bps. Data: `env.data["spreads"]` (a `SpreadsLog`, real) or four synthetic fortnights from `SpreadModel` (placeholders) → PROVISIONAL. Reported too: the share of ticks whose worst-pair spread exceeds the value in calm and around the `crash-70-1d` ramp (a MINT-10 refusal proxy) |
| `pinDeltaBps` | PIN-1 arming rate over rolling `pinWindow` windows of hourly history (`env.data["price"]` if real, else 90-day GARCH paths): > 20 % confirm; < 5 % (`pin_low_move_fraction`) drop to 200–300 — the README's "smaller of the two whose rate clears 20 %", read literally (200 if neither clears); in between keep ("the proposal biases larger") |

## Other families

| Param(s) | Kind | Metrics / constraints |
|---|---|---|
| `kSlack` | optimize: min bundle bytes `4 + 74(m+k)` | `unavail` = 1 − beta-binomial liveness in the per-attestor freshness `1−(1−u)²` (u = `attestor_uptime`, ρ = `attestor_outage_correlation`) ≤ `max_attest_unavailability`; `unavail_outage1` (one equal-weight seat down, hypergeometric mixture); `dead_detectable` (k ≥ 1: with no slack a dead selected attestor fails every bundle that picks it, so no row ever lists it — confirmed by `attest.simulate`) |
| `mSelect`, `nSlots` | verify | liveness; **harmful capture** ≤ 0.01: exact-kernel Monte Carlo (`attest.capture_probability`, W9 selection + weighted quantile) of an entity holding `max_single_entity_weight_share` of the seated weight in one seat moving aMint **up** 10 %; `nSlots ≥ attestArmMin` |

| `qLowBps` (+`qHighBps` derived) | verify | harmful capture ≤ 0.01 and **griefing** (the entity spans qLow of a bundle → aMint down 10 %) ≤ 0.05 — the proposal §7.2 rule "Q_LOW must exceed the largest single-entity weight share among the *selected* attestors", evaluated at bundle level |
| `attestInterval` (+`attestMaxAge` = 2k, locked) | verify | relay 1/k ≤ `attest_relay_budget_per_block`; p99 price move over `attestMaxAge` (`2.326·σ·√(maxAge/N)`) ≤ divergeBpsAttest/3; liveness. Markov (48-block outages) liveness reported as sensitivity |
| `attestArmMin` | verify | an equal founding set gives no founder ≥ qLow (`armMin·qLow > 10⁴`); bundles exist with that many seats |
| `attestArmDelay`, `foundingWindow` | verify | `attestArmDelay ≤ foundingWindow` (founders in the delay keep the founding age) |
| `attestRequired` | design choice (W15) | verified true, never tuned |
| `dormancyBlocks`, `dormancyMinBundles` | optimize: min p95 blocks to eject a dead seated attestor (Monte Carlo of selected rows at the adoption case's bundle rate, rows only when the others still make the bundle) | honest false ejection/yr (`false_dormancy_probability`, union bound) ≤ `max_false_ejection_prob`; dead detection ≤ `max_dead_detection_blocks`. | `dormancyCheck` | verify | same constraints |
| `bondMin` | optimize: **max** griefing capital (USD, p05 of the worst price over a year) — the aged bond an attacker needs to hold qLow of a bundle against `m+k−1` minimum bonds | an honest bond's monthly opportunity cost (`bond_opportunity_cost_apr`) ≤ `attestor_min_monthly_revenue_usd`. Seq exhaustion (u16) reported |
| `bondMinLock` | verify | fixed at `BLOCKS_PER_YEAR` (§1.4) |
| `bondMaturity` | verify | ≥ one week of public notice before a bond counts |
| `ageCap` | verify | a capital-matched newcomer needs ≥ 90 days to reach the harmful share `(1−qLow)/qLow·ageCap`; an honest newcomer is seated within a year |

pEmerg is modelled as the attestors' price (true price `maxAge/2` blocks old; `min(xClaim, aClaim) ≤
aClaim` and the pool medians lag much more); vaults sit 5 % above `emergencyRatio` before the event.

**Simulation confirmation** (in `decide`, `attest.simulate`, current and recommended sets): bundle
success rate of an all-honest seated set vs the analytic liveness; a dead attestor's ejection time
vs the Monte-Carlo model (same row model, both at one bundle per hour). Stored under
`Recommendation.metrics["simulation"]`.

## Provenance

`divergeBpsAttest`, `pin*`, `bondMin` (price paths) and `emergencyPersist` (scenario paths) are
`synthetic` without real data → PROVISIONAL. Liveness, capture, arming, dormancy, ages and verify
families are exact math under policy/judgement assumptions → `judgement`.

## Judgement constants (`g8_attestation.JUDGEMENT`)

`mean_outage_blocks` 48, `capture_prob_max` 0.01, `grief_prob_max` 0.05, `capture_move_bps` 1,000,
`stale_share_of_diverge` 1/3, `premature_claim_max` 0.01, `vault_buffer` 0.05,
`claim_reaction_blocks` 576, `registration_notice_blocks` 8,064, `min_capture_days` 90,
`max_newcomer_seat_days` 365, `yec_vol_annual` 1.2. The honest feed for false pins refreshes at the
slowest `SpreadModel` source's rate (fitted when a spreads log is given); the honest pool is the
smallest `PoolModel` share (4 %).

## How real data changes the answer

- Two weeks of `spreads.py log` → `env.data["spreads"]`: the divergeBpsAttest target becomes the
  real 3×p95 (the synthetic SpreadModel defaults give worst p95 ≈ 358 bps → 1,100).
- ≥ 90 days of hourly YEC/USD → `env.data["price"]`: the arming rate (synthetic GARCH: 9.7 % at
  500 bps, inconclusive → keep), the bond's USD value and staleness volatility become real.
- The adoption case drives PIN arming and dormancy: at `adoption_case = "low"` (2 mints/day) a
  pinWindow holds ≥ 2 bundle rows only 9 % of the time, and a dead attestor collects ~18.7
  selected rows per 14-day window — fewer than `dormancyMinBundles` 20.

and spans qLow in 94 % of bundles (griefing; theft probability 0). `dormancyMinBundles` 20 → 12: at
