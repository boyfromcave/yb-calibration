# G6 — miner judgement and fees

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g6_miners_fees.py` (WP-7d). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.6](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g6_miners_fees.py`. Decisions: D-WP7d-1, -2, -3, -6, -7 in
[decisions.md](../decisions.md).

Owned parameters (`params_for_group("G6")`): locked `peerLag`, `peerMin`, `deviationBps`,
`accuracyBandBps`, `payeeWindow`, `feeMin`, `feeBps`, `attestFeeBps`; excluded (patch-release)
`nPenalty`, `accuracyWindow`, `payeeTiltBps` (L6 wallet defaults) and `nReg` (informational, REG-1).

## Structure

Like G5/G8, the study is a set of **families** (`g5_activation.Family` / `FamilyStudy`) on one table of
one-at-a-time sweeps around the current set, plus a 2-D grid for `feeBps × attestFeeBps` (the two fee
rates trade against each other and are decided together). Kinds: **optimize** (a primary metric with
policy constraints through `decide_with_materiality`: KEEP unless the gain exceeds `materiality`, ties
toward current, a violating current is always changed), **verify** (KEEP unless a constraint fails, then
the nearest value that passes) and **rule** (a closed-form target; KEEP within `materiality` of it).
Every verdict goes through `final_verdict`, so synthetic-only evidence is PROVISIONAL.

## Models

### REG-4 judgement streams (`judge_stream`)

For each scenario `calm-90d`, `crash-70-1d`, `pump-dump-3x`, `stale-pools` (block resolution, horizon
`g1.horizon_days`, `g1.paths_for(budget)` paths):

1. True prices from the scenario (a demeaned bootstrap of real returns when `env.data["price"]` is real).
2. Exchange sources from `SpreadModel` (fitted to `env.data["spreads"]` when present; else the three
   placeholder sources: bias 0/+40/−30 bps, s.d. 40/120/150 bps, AR(1) persistence 30 min, correlated,
   held between Poisson refreshes, with outages).
3. Pools from `PoolModel` (fitted to `env.data["pool_shares"]`/`["hashrate"]`; else six pools with 80 %
   of the hash: 25/20/15/10/6/4 %). Block miners are drawn by share; pool `i` quotes the 12-block TWAP
   (the agent's 15 minutes) of source `i mod 3`, `lag_blocks` old, times its agent noise; a pool in an
   outage writes no quote.
4. `stale-pools` from day 5: the pools whose shares sum closest to 30 % quote a 48-block-old TWAP
   (honest but slow, kind 1); the pool nearest 10 % repeats one quote (frozen, kind 2).

`reg4_deviations(tag_price, peerLag)` is REG-4 (state.cpp:910-936) returning the deviation itself:
peers = quote tags at `[t − lag, t + lag − 1]` other than `t`, `m` their lower median,
`dev = |q − m|·10⁴ // m`. A test pins it to `ybcal.sim.fees.judgement_series` (evaluated ⇔
`count ≥ peerMin` and the judgement height inside the series; in band ⇔ `dev ≤ accuracyBandBps`;
penalised ⇔ `dev > deviationBps`).

### Fee book (`revenue_book`)

One hour-mode vault book (`simulate_vault_book_hours`) over 365 days from `startHeight`, `book_paths`
(= `budget.paths // 4`) GARCH(1,1)-t placeholder paths with the log drift removed (or a real
bootstrap), personas from `agents_config(policy)` = `AgentsConfig.from_policy` plus the `[agents]`
keys (`yed_premium_bps`, `claimant_slippage_bps`, `defector_share`, `lost_key_prob`). The book is
run once with every G6 parameter at its registry value (hour mode reads none of them: E(R) is never
empty there); FEE-1 and AFEE-1 are then recomputed exactly (`vkernels.fee_zat`, `attest_fee_zat`) on
the book's vaults for every candidate — common random numbers, and a fee does not change who mints.
The book includes the MINT-6 refusals of fact 1.5-2, so revenue reflects the classes that actually
get in.

## Metrics (precise definitions)

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `judge.honest_p99` | max over the four scenarios of the p99 of `dev` over evaluated tags of normal honest pools (kind 0) |
| `judge.honest_p75_calm` | p75 of the same in `calm-90d` |
| `judge.false_penalty` | max over scenarios of the share of evaluated normal-honest tags with `dev > deviationBps` |
| `judge.false_penalty_stale` | the same for the stale pools of `stale-pools` (honest but slow) |
| `judge.frozen_penalised` | the same for the frozen pool (reported: how fast REG-4 notices a stuck feed) |
| `judge.not_evaluated` | share of quote tags (judgement height inside the series) with fewer than `peerMin` peers, `calm-90d` |
| `judge.not_evaluated_analytic` | `P(Bin(2·peerLag − 1, d) < peerMin)`, `d` = quote-tag density of the calm stream |
| `judge.liar_detect_Y` | calm: share of evaluated honest tags that, moved by ±Y bps (half up, half down) against the same peers, have `dev > deviationBps` (a liar's own other tags among the peers are ignored — a small pool is a small minority) |
| `payee.fee0` | share of heights R in calm with no quote tag in `(R − payeeWindow, R]` (FEE-0: the mint pays no fee) |
| `payee.small_pool_in_payees` | `1 − (1 − r)^payeeWindow`, `r` = quote tags per block of the smallest pool |
| `reg.registered` | `1 − (1 − r)^nReg` (REG-1 for the smallest pool) |
| `pen.honest_excluded` | `1 − (1 − r_eq·fp)^nPenalty`: share of time an equal-share honest pool (`r_eq` its quote tags per block) is excluded from FEE-W by a false penalty at rate `fp = judge.false_penalty` |
| `pen.liar_excluded` | `1 − (1 − r)^nPenalty`: share of time a liar with the smallest share (penalised on every tag) is excluded |
| `accw.sd_bps` | `10⁴·√(p(1−p)/N)`, `N` = judged tags of an equal-share pool in `accuracyWindow`, `p` = honest in-band share in calm |
| `tilt.honest_spread` | max/min FEE-W weight `10⁴ + tilt·accuracyBps/10⁴` over the honest pools' calm accuracies |
| `tilt.accuracy_premium` | weight of an honest pool at the median accuracy over an accuracy-0 pool, − 1 |
| `fee.share_minmint` | max over classes of the round-trip fee (mint FEE-1 + AFEE-1, redeem FEE-1) of a `minMint` vault as a share of its debt (`fees.fee_table`, ARMED, σ = 1, reference price) |
| `fee.pool_usd_month` | book FEE-1 (mints at the true price at the mint, owner redeems/claims at the close) per path-day × 30 / `expected_pool_count` |
| `fee.attestor_usd_month` | AFEE-1 on every accepted mint and every claim (an ARMED chain) per path-day × 30 / `nSlots` |
| `fee.pool_required_usd` | `max(pool_min_monthly_revenue_usd, pool_operating_cost_usd_month)` |
| `fee.attestor_required_usd` | `max(attestor_min_monthly_revenue_usd, bondMin × reference price × bond_opportunity_cost_apr / 12)` |
| `fee.edge_fee_share_max` | max over classes and prices {reference, `worst_price_usd`} of FEE-1 / collateral of a `minMint` vault (wallet rounding, `4·feeMin` floor) |
| `fee.edge_fee_share_limit` | `1 − 10⁴/claimThresholdBps`: the margin a vault keeps when its value falls to the claim threshold |

Reference price: the last real price when `env.data["price"]` is real, else the scenario library's
$0.40.

## Decision rules

| Param | Family / kind | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `peerLag` | optimize | min `judge.honest_p99` s.t. `not_evaluated` ≤ `max_not_evaluated_prob` (at the current peerMin) and `false_penalty` ≤ `max_false_penalty_rate` |
| `peerMin` | optimize (max) | the **largest** value with `not_evaluated` ≤ `max_not_evaluated_prob` (PLAN). If `peerLag` changes, `adjust_changes` re-checks the chosen peerMin at the new lag with the analytic binomial and lowers it if needed |
| `deviationBps` | optimize (min of the value) | as small as allowed by `dev ≥ k_dev × judge.honest_p99`, false penalties (normal and stale pools) ≤ `max_false_penalty_rate`, and a ±`liar_bias_bps` (2,000) liar caught on ≥ `min_liar_detection` (0.90) of its tags |
| `accuracyBandBps` | rule | `judge.honest_p75_calm` rounded to 50 bps (bounds 100–1,000) |
| `payeeWindow` | verify | `payee.fee0` ≤ `max_fee0_prob` (0.001) |
| `feeMin` | verify | `4·feeMin` ≤ the collateral of a `minMint` class-C vault at `worst_price_usd` (§1.4) and `fee.edge_fee_share_max` ≤ `fee.edge_fee_share_limit` (the owner of a minMint vault at the claim edge still gains by redeeming — the PLAN's "redemption affordability under the 4·feeMin floor at crash prices") |
| `feeBps`, `attestFeeBps` | optimize on the 2-D grid | min `fee.share_minmint` s.t. `fee_share` ≤ `max_fee_share_small`, pool and attestor revenue ≥ their requirements under `adoption_case`. If no grid point passes: **BLOCKED** at the least-violating point (minimum sum of relative violations `viol.*`, ties toward current) plus a design note |
| `nPenalty` (L6) | optimize | min `pen.honest_excluded` s.t. `pen.liar_excluded` ≥ `min_liar_exclusion` (0.99) and `pen.honest_excluded` ≤ `max_honest_exclusion` (0.01) |
| `accuracyWindow` (L6) | verify | `accw.sd_bps` ≤ `max_accuracy_sd_bps` (500) |
| `payeeTiltBps` (L6) | verify | `tilt.honest_spread` ≤ `max_honest_payee_spread` (1.5) and `tilt.accuracy_premium` ≥ `min_accuracy_premium` (0.25) |
| `nReg` (informational) | verify | `reg.registered` ≥ `min_registered_prob` (0.999) |

Constants in parentheses are `g6_miners_fees.JUDGEMENT` values; each is read as
`getattr(policy, key, default)` so the policy keys requested in D-WP7d-6 take over once added.

## Design notes raised

`design_notes(results, policy)` (also in every G6 `Recommendation.metrics["design_notes"]`):

* **FEE-1 base.** FEE-1 is a share of the collateral, so the round-trip fee as a share of the debt is
  `2·feeBps·baseRatio` (+ AFEE-1) at every size: class A pays 5/3 of class C's share. With
  `max_fee_share_small` = 2 % class A caps feeBps at ≈ 17 bps ARMED. A debt-based FEE-1 would be
  class-neutral (rule change).
* **Fee incentives** (when the fee family is BLOCKED). AFEE-1 is a fraction of FEE-1; at the low
  adoption case it cannot cover a seated attestor's bond opportunity cost without pushing class A over
  the fee-share cap.

## Assumptions

* Quote noise, staleness and outages are the `SpreadModel` / `PoolModel` placeholders; deviations scale
  directly with the source dispersion.
* Liar detection ignores the liar's own tags among the peers (conservative for a small liar only).
* Revenue per pool is an equal split over `expected_pool_count` (FEE-W picks payees in proportion to
  their tags, so a pool's expected share is its tag share; the smallest pool earns proportionally less).
* AFEE-1 is counted on every mint (an ARMED chain); before arming attestors earn nothing.
* The book's sizes follow the minter's log-uniform $100–$10,000 distribution at the adoption case's mint
  rate; MINT-6 refusals (fact 1.5-2) reduce the paying mints.

## How real data changes the answer

* `env.data["spreads"]` (a `spreads.py` log, two weeks or more): the honest deviation distribution
  — and with it `deviationBps`, `accuracyBandBps`, `peerLag` and the L6 values — moves with the real
  source dispersion. Judgement provenance becomes `real-data` only with a real price path as well.
* `env.data["pool_shares"]`: real pool count and shares set `P(not evaluated)`, the smallest pool's tag
  rate (`payeeWindow`, `nReg`, `nPenalty`) and the quote density.
* `env.data["price"]` (real hourly YEC/USD): the revenue book bootstraps real returns and quotes fees at
  the real price; `fee.attestor_required_usd` follows the real price through the bond's USD value.
* The fee decision is dominated by the policy (adoption case, revenue floors, `max_fee_share_small`):
  owner edits there move it more than data does.

## Wave 2 on real data (2026-10-04, D-RD-ATT-1, -2, -6, -7, -8)

* **Honest quotes are the shipped agent's** (`pool_feed = "agent"`, default): every tagging pool
  publishes `ybcal.sim.feeds.agent_quotes` of the venue quotes — per-venue 12-block window average,
  silence drop, 10 % outlier filter, median, fail closed below `agent_min_sources` (a fail-closed pool
  writes a signal-only tag). Venues are the real per-venue deviations from the CoinGecko aggregate
  (`VenueReplay`, from `env.data["spreads"]`). `pool_feed = "venue"` restores the WP-7d one-venue model.
* **New metrics**: `judge.venue_penalised.<venue>` (an honest tag replaced by that venue's TWAP, calm),
  `judge.agent_fail_closed`, and the adversarial scenarios `adv.<scenario>.honest_fp / not_evaluated /
  rogue_penalised / venue_penalised / venue_in_band` for `rogue-major-pool-{15,5}`,
  `major-pool-offline`, `venue-pool` (`ADV_SCENARIOS`; never part of the honest p99).
* **nPenalty** is judged at the calm steady state; the stale-pools exclusion is
  `pen.honest_excluded_stress`; exclusions below a tenth of the budget count as zero.
* **Fees**: `attestFeeBps` is owner-pinned (D-3, `OWNER_PINNED` ∪ policy `owner_pinned`): the grid is
  searched at the pinned value and the free optimum is a note. When the attestor floor and the fee-share
  cap cannot both hold, the most attestor revenue under the cap is chosen (environment limit
  G6-ENV-1, `metrics["environment_blocked"]`).
