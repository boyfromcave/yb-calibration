# G9 — amounts and wallet policy (`minMint`, `maxMint`, `minOutput`, `maxOutput`, `residualMinZat`; excluded `carrierValue`, `walletConfirmations`, `DEFAULT_REF_LAG`)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g9_amounts.py` (WP-7b). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.9](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g9_amounts.py`. Decisions: D-WP7b-5.

## Method: admissible ranges, verify rule

Every G9 constraint is a closed-form bound, so the study computes each parameter's admissible range
`[lo, hi]`, rounds it onto the registry lattice (lower bounds up, upper bounds down) and applies a
**verify** rule (D-WP7b-5): KEEP the current value while it lies inside; else the nearest admissible
value (CHANGE); BLOCKED when the range is empty. A value is not moved just because another one is also
admissible (PLAN §2.3). The amount order `minOutput ≤ minMint ≤ maxMint ≤ maxOutput` is enforced by
resolving `maxMint → maxOutput → minMint → minOutput`, each against the already recommended
neighbours. The candidate table only carries one-at-a-time sweeps for the sensitivity sentences.

| Param | Lower bound | Upper bound |
|---|---|---|
| `minMint` | max(`4·feeMin` collateral at `worst_price_usd` in the lowest-ratio class — the MINT-5 floor invariant, $66.67 at the defaults; the size below which the `feeMin` floor pushes the round-trip fee above max(`max_fee_share_small`, the class's proportional share `2·feeBps·ratio`) at the reference price) | `maxMint` |
| `maxMint` | `minMint` | `max_depth_fraction × p10 daily YEC volume ÷ θ` (one vault's liquidation at the claim threshold) — only with a volume series (`env.data["depth"].volume_24h_usd`) or the policy key `yec_daily_volume_p10_usd`; else `maxOutput` and the study reports the volume the current value needs ($110,000/day at $10,000 and 10 %) |
| `minOutput` | `dust_spend_multiple` (3) × the 1,000-zat network fee at `worst_price_usd` | `minMint` |
| `maxOutput` | `maxMint` | registry bound |
| `residualMinZat` | max(`dust_zat`, 3 × network fee) = 3,000 zat | `residual_max_share` (1 %) of a minMint debt at the worst price |
| `carrierValue` | max(`dust_zat`, 3 × network fee) | `carrier_max_share` (0.1 %) of a minMint debt at the worst price |
| `walletConfirmations` | smallest `z` with Nakamoto's catch-up probability (Bitcoin paper §11) at `reorg_attacker_share` ≤ `max_reorg_prob` (24 at 30 % / 0.1 %) | registry bound (30) |
| `DEFAULT_REF_LAG` | smallest lag with P(VOID by a natural reorg deeper than the lag) = `orphan_rate^(lag+1)` ≤ `max_void_prob` | `refWindow − mint_inclusion_slack_blocks` (MINT-2 must still hold when the mint confirms) |

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `fee_share.{c}@ref` / `@worst` | WP-4 `fees.fee_table`: round-trip FEE-1 (mint + owner redeem) / debt for a minMint vault at σ = 1, at the reference price (real median of the last 30 days, else the synthetic $0.40, or `reference_price_usd`) / the worst price |
| `prop_share.{c}` | `2·feeBps·ratio`: the size-free share above the floor (2.5 / 2.0 / 1.5 %) |
| `fee.floor_binds_above_usd` | YEC price above which `feeMin`, not `feeBps`, sets a minMint vault's fee |
| `maxMint.liquidation_usd`, `volume_needed_usd`, `liquidation_share` | `maxMint × θ`; that ÷ `max_depth_fraction`; that ÷ p10 volume |
| `reorg.p_catch_up` | Nakamoto catch-up probability at `walletConfirmations` |
| `void.p_natural`, `void.p_attacker` | `orphan_rate^(lag+1)`; catch-up probability from `lag + 1` blocks (reported, not constrained: a VOID is a nuisance — the collateral is released — not a loss) |
| `residual.usd_at_worst`, `carrier.usd_at_worst` | value at the worst price |

## Findings this study reports rather than tunes

- **G9-DN1**: FEE-1 is charged on the collateral, so the round-trip share of the debt is
  `2·feeBps·ratio` at every size — 2.5 % for class A, above `max_fee_share_small` (2 %). No `minMint`
  fixes a proportional fee; the levers are G6's `feeBps` or a rule change (fee on the debt).
- **G9-DN2**: `feeMin` is in YEC: above $1.50/YEC the floor sets a minMint vault's fee, and at the
  policy's worst price ($100) the round-trip share of a $100 debt is 100 %.

## Provenance

`minMint` follows the reference price (synthetic → PROVISIONAL), `maxMint` the volume source (none →
synthetic/PROVISIONAL with the needed volume in the notes), the rest are `judgement` (exact math under
the policy's assumptions). Excluded parameters carry the patch-release note.

## How real data changes the answer

A real price moves the reference price (and the floor-binding price); a depth/volume CSV turns the
`maxMint` bound on (with 10 % of a $20,000 p10 volume the bound is $1,818 → $1,000). A measured
attacker share or orphan rate changes `walletConfirmations` / `DEFAULT_REF_LAG` directly.
