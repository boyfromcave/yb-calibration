# Seats — attestor seat ranking, eviction and the bond minimum

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/attest_seats.py` on the exact mirrors in `src/ybcal/model/seats.py`. CLI:
`ybcal seats [--out DIR]`. Tests: `tests/studies/test_attest_seats.py`, `tests/model/test_seats.py`.
Report: [docs/reports/2026-10-seats/](../reports/2026-10-seats/README.md). Decisions: quantum plan D-Q-29
(open set, log tenure, eviction), D-Q-30 (collateral-based bond minimum, exit any time, 14-day return),
D-Q-31 (slashing reaches a bond in its exit delay, ≥ 14-day exit window).

An analytic study, not a group study: no price pulls, no simulation engine, no devnet; it runs in a few
seconds. It changes no registry parameter.

The price statistic and W9 selection keep `BondWeight` = bond × min(age, AGE_CAP)
(`kernels.bond_weight`); only seats and eviction use the tenure factor.

**Exactness.** The vendored reference model is still ycash6 `7702d22` (v3), which predates these rules, so
the mirrors are transcribed from the C++ and held to 2,428 vectors computed by the node's own Python
model (`qa/rpc-tests/test_framework/yellowback_model.py` at `4bb710b4c`; `src/ybcal/model/seats_vectors.json`,
regenerated by `python -m ybcal.model.gen_seats_vectors <ycash-dd tree> <out>`) and to the C++ unit-test
values of `yellowback_math_tests.cpp` `seat_weight_log_tenure_factor`. `ybcal verify` checks them
(section `seats`).

## Method

Six tables, all exact integer evaluations: the tenure factor and the bond multiples it implies; the days
from joining to a seat for a newcomer of `r` × an incumbent's bond (hourly scan over three years, both
tenures advancing, newcomer ELIGIBLE at 14 days); the least bond that evicts a live member by bond and
tenure; who holds the nine seats for mixes of 20,000-YEC and 5,000-YEC bonders, and the capital to take 7
(harmful capture, D-RD-ATT-4) or 9 seats from an honest set; a 30-day trace of an entrant joining one
bond a day into a full set of 5,000-YEC members; and the D-Q-30 minimum against vault collateral.
Dollars at the $0.40 reference (frozen 2026-10-04 data).

## Not covered

The block-mode simulator (`sim/attest.py`) and G8 still seat by the v3 rule (bond × min(age, AGE_CAP),
no hysteresis, no eviction) of the pinned ycash6 `7702d22`; their parity tests run against that pin.
Moving them needs the vendored model re-pinned to a ycash6 commit carrying `q/attest`, which does not
exist yet (ycash6 `q/attest` = `upgrade/quantum-vaults`). G8's `age.newcomer_seat_days` metric therefore
describes the old rule: under D-Q-29 an equal-bond newcomer is never seated by ranking alone.
