# G4 — grace and abandonment (`grace`, `abandonBlocks`)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g4_grace_abandon.py` (WP-7b). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.4](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g4_grace_abandon.py`. Decisions: D-WP7b-4.

## What the parameters control

- **`grace`** — MINT-2/3: the claim path opens at `claimHeight = lockHeight + grace`. It is the
  owner's window to redeem after the lock ends and, because nobody may claim before it (fact 1.5-1),
  extra drawdown exposure the base ratio must cover.
- **`abandonBlocks`** — L10/L12 (index.cpp:732): ENFORCEMENT set at every snapshot of the last
  `abandonBlocks` blocks = the module is abandoned; templates (TPL-1/2), the mempool (MP-1) and
  `yed_sweep` then let owners take their collateral back without burning. W21: `abandonBlocks ≥
  grace`; PLAN §5.4 proposes `abandonBlocks ≥` the freeze-then-fix runbook length.

## Method and metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `owner.p_miss` | WP-4 `agents.p_owner_miss(grace, owner)`: P(an honest owner is absent through `[lock, lock + grace]`), `1 − exp(−λ·E[(D − G)⁺])`, absence spells Poisson at `owner_absence_rate_per_year`, log-normal lengths (`owner_absence_median_days`, `owner_absence_sigma`). Lost keys excluded (no grace helps them) and reported as `owner.p_miss_with_lost_keys` |
| `dP.{A,B,C}` | WP-4 fast path on G3's hour ensemble with every grace of the lattice in one call per member: `P(bad at lock + grace) − P(bad at lock)`, term-grid mean, aggregated over members with `ensemble_agg` (worst) |
| `dP.weighted` | `0.4·dP.A + 0.4·dP.B + 0.2·dP.C` (the default demand mix, `MinterConfig.class_weights`) |
| `J` | `w_owner · p_miss + w_debt · dP.weighted` |
| `abandon.runbook` | `signalWindow` (detect, upper bound) + `signalWindow` (W19 halted window) + `release_lead_blocks` (M14, 16,128) + `runbook_operator_buffer_blocks` (+ `dev_absence_tolerance_days` if the policy sets it) = 24,768 at the defaults |
| `abandon.lb`, `abandon.margin` | `max(grace, runbook)`; `abandonBlocks − lb` |
| `abandon.false_prob` | G5 `false_abandon_probability(params, shares, A)`: upper bound on P(ENFORCEMENT held `A` blocks within a year with no real abandonment), over G5's drift-mixture share samples at `expected_enforcing_share` (also at a 60 % share) |
| `dev.premature_{14,30,60,90}d` | 1 if abandonment fires before developers absent that long can ship a fixed set: halt-to-fix = absence + max(0, W − absence) + lead + buffer > A |
| `dev.max_absence_days` | the longest developer absence the current `A` tolerates: `(A − lead − buffer) / 1,152` |
| `dev.unsweepable_days_180d` | developers truly gone: days vaults stay unsweepable after the halt = `A / 1,152` |

## Decision rules

- **grace** = argmin J on a one-day lattice (quick: three days, anchored on the current value) subject
  to `P(owner miss) ≤ max_owner_miss_prob`; `decide_with_materiality` (KEEP unless J improves by more
  than `materiality`; a violating current value moves to the best feasible grace); BLOCKED
  (least-violating) if no grace meets the owner tolerance. The debt side has no tolerance of its own
  (the policy's bad-debt tolerance is on the *total*, which G3 meets with the ratios); "the grace window
  alone exceeds the class's whole budget" (`dP.c > max_bad_debt_prob[c]`) is reported as a flag in
  `metrics["flags"]` and as design note G4-DN2 (D-WP7b-4).
- **abandonBlocks** = the smallest value ≥ `max(recommended grace, runbook)` with
  `false_prob ≤ max_false_abandon_prob`, rounded up to whole days (closed form, computed in `decide`
  at the recommended grace). KEEP unless the current value violates the bound or the rule value is
  more than `materiality` smaller; when grace moves, the provenance follows grace's.

## Design notes

**G4-DN1** abandonment tolerates only `(A − lead − buffer)` days of developer absence (12 days at the
shipped 30 days) — a longer `A` protects slow developers but keeps vaults locked when they are truly
gone; set `dev_absence_tolerance_days` to buy tolerance explicitly. **G4-DN2** the grace window is pure
drawdown exposure (fact 1.5-1): a rule allowing RED-4 claims from `lockHeight` would remove the
trade-off.

## Assumptions

- The absence model is the policy's (one spell a year, median 7 days, σ 1); the `owner-absence`
  scenario's holiday dip is not added (it shifts P(miss) uniformly).
- Detection is taken at its upper bound (`signalWindow`), as PLAN §5.4 states; G5 reports the p95.
- The false-abandonment bound assumes iid signalling at a drifting share (G5 D-WP7c-3).

## How real data changes the answer

Real prices move `dP` (the debt side of J); a pool-share log in `env.data["pool_shares"]` makes the
false-abandonment term `real-data`. The owner-miss side is policy (the absence model) until owner
behaviour data exists.
