# G1 — price medians (`pFastWindow`, `pMidWindow`, `pSlowWindow`, min-fills)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g1_price_windows.py` (WP-7a). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.1](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g1_price_windows.py`.

## What the parameters control

PRICE-1 (state.cpp:1197-1211) takes three **lower medians with min-fill** over the coinbase quote
tags of the last `W` blocks: pFast (W = 96, fill ⌈W/2⌉ = 48), pMid (576, ⌈2W/3⌉ = 384), pSlow
(2,016, 1,344). A median with fewer quotes than its fill is undefined.

- **pMint = min(pFast, pMid, pSlow)** — the minting price. It follows a fall at the speed of
  pFast and a rise at the speed of the slowest window.
- **pClaim = max(pMid, pSlow)** — the claim / underwater price (RED-4). It follows a fall at the
  speed of the slowest window.
- **NO_PRICE** — pMint undefined (any median below its fill).
- **HALT-3** — pFast < pMid·(1 − divergenceBps) or pMid < pSlow·(1 − divergenceBps); falls only.
- **SIGMA-1** measures volatility on pFast (G2 coupling); MINT-10 compares attestations with it.

Min-fills are derived (L9). The study tunes the windows; each fill's Recommendation reports the
derived value.

## Method

1. **Scenarios** (block resolution; `env.scenarios` entries of the same name win, else the shipped
   library): `calm-90d`, `crash-70-1d`, `crash-90-30d`, `pump-dump-3x`, `flash-wick-50-1h`,
   `feed-outage-6h`, `oracle-attack-34`. Horizon = the budget's block horizon, extended to the
   scenario's last programmed event + 6 days, capped at the scenario's own horizon. Calm uses 3× the
   paths over ≥ 60 days (rare-event metric); the crashes 2× (tail metric).
2. **True prices**: the scenario's synthetic base, or — when `env.data["price"]` is a *real*
   `PricePath` — a demeaned stationary block bootstrap of the real returns as the scenario base
   (`Scenario.generate(base_path=…)`), so the program (crash, pump) rides on real noise.
3. **Quote tags**: WP-3 `oracle.generate_block_inputs`, quote = 15-minute TWAP × (1 + 30 bps
   noise), and **independent pool feed outages** (each pool 1 per 30 days, exponential length,
   mean 4 h, signal-only while out). Pools: with a **pool-share log** loaded (`--data
   pool-shares.csv`), the real landscape (D-RD-ORA-1) — its keys ≥ 1 % (largest 8) at their real
   shares, the real block-by-block **miner sequence replayed** from a random offset per path (so the
   day-to-day swings of real shares reach the fills), and the tagging set from the policy key
   `tagging_pools` (payout-key prefixes; the real-data policy names the three identified pools,
   71.9 %), else the largest keys until `expected_enforcing_share`. Without a log: the policy's
   `expected_pool_count` equal pools at `expected_enforcing_share`. The scenario's `tagging_share` /
   `feed_up` schedules thin the quotes (a 6-hour feed outage removes all of them). The attack adds a
   coalition of hash `attack_share_min` carved out of the honest pools, biasing quotes by ±10 %
   (scenario constant) over the attack window; its clean counterpart has the same pools and draws.
4. None of this depends on G1's parameters, so it is generated once per run and shared by every
   candidate (common random numbers). PRICE-1 for any `(W, fill)` comes from one
   `vkernels.RollingMedian` per realisation (memoised per process, medians cached up to 768 MB).
   A test asserts these medians, pMint, pClaim, HALT-3 and NO_PRICE equal
   `sim.engine.simulate_blocks` on the same inputs.
5. All metrics skip the first 4,032 blocks (the longest searchable slow window), the start-up
   period in which medians are undefined because their windows have not filled.

## Metrics (per candidate)

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `attack_share_{fast,mid,slow}`, `attack_share_min` | V16, analytic: smallest coalition share `s` for which the coalition's quotes are the lower median of the window with probability ≥ 0.5 (`oracle.attack_success_prob`, exact binomial over the window; honest quoting share = tagging share − `s`, the rest untagged), min over up/down; `attack_share_min` = min over the three windows |
| `attack_moved_mint_up`, `attack_moved_claim_down` | simulated, at `attack_share_min` with ±10 % bias: fraction of attack blocks in which pMint is ≥ 5 % above / pClaim ≥ 5 % below the clean run (the harmful directions: over-minting, premature claims) |
| `attack_moved_fast_{up,down}`, `attack_extra_halt3_h` | the same for pFast (griefing: minting price pushed down, HALT-3 provoked) |
| `lag_{mint,claim}{50,90}_{crash70,crash90}_h` | mean hours by which pMint / pClaim lag the true price in covering 50 % / 90 % of the crash (move from the median true price over the hour before the ramp to the median over the day after it; lag = first block the oracle price crosses − first block the true price crosses, ≥ 0; never → censored at the horizon) |
| `crash_lag_cvar_h` (**primary**) | CVaR at `crash_lag_cvar_alpha` (95 %) over all crash paths of the pClaim 90 % lag, hours |
| `pump_overpricing_bps` | E[max(pMint − true, 0)/true] over the pump-dump scenario from the top (dump start) to one day after the dump, bps (undefined pMint = no mint = 0) |
| `exposure_{crash70,crash90}_h` | mean hours per path with pClaim > true price, from the crash start to two days after its end |
| `no_price_h_per_year` | NO_PRICE hours per year in calm with pool outages (background availability) |
| `no_price_h_per_feed_outage` | NO_PRICE hours caused by one 6-hour outage of every feed |
| `halt3_recall_{crash70,crash90}`, `halt3_delay_*_h` | fraction of paths in which HALT-3 fires within one day of the crash start; mean delay |
| `halt3_false_h_per_year`, `halt3_pump_dump_h`, `halt3_wick_h` | HALT-3 hours: calm (annualised), whole pump-dump, the day after a 1-hour 50 % wick |
| `wick_mint_drop_bps` | mean largest pMint drop within 6 h of the wick, relative to the hour before |

| `tagging_share`, `top_pool_share`, `top_pool_quote_share` | the simulated tagging share; the largest tagging key's share of blocks and of quotes (real landscape) |
| `top_pool_control_{fast,mid,slow}`, `top_pool_control_min`, `attack_env_blocked` | P(that key's quotes are the lower median) per window at its real share (V16 exact binomial, worse direction); `attack_env_blocked` = 1 when ≥ ½ in every window — it sets every median alone, whatever the windows |
| `no_price_seq_h_per_year` | NO_PRICE on the real miner sequence with the policy's tagging set, no feed outages (exact: the fill counts quotes only) |
| `no_price_all_tag_h_per_year`, `no_price_top{2,3,4}_h_per_year` | the same if every named key / exactly the 2, 3, 4 largest keys tag (adoption ladder) |
| `no_price_top_withholds_h_per_year` | the same if the largest tagging key keeps mining but stops quoting |
| `rogue_{up,down,grief}_capture_h_{fast,mid,slow}` | `rogue-pool-52-*` (D-RD-ORA-4): the largest real pool quotes +10 % / −10 % / −30 % from day 5 for 20 days on the real miner sequence; median hours until each median sits half the bias off the clean run |
| `rogue_up_mint_share`, `rogue_down_claim_share`, `rogue_*_halt3_share` | share of the attack's blocks with pMint ≥ 5 % above / pClaim ≥ 5 % below the clean run (the harm before the attestation layer arms) and with HALT-3 |
| `rogue_withhold_noprice_share`, `_onset_h`, `_recovery_h` | the pool mines but stops quoting for 10 days: NO_PRICE share, hours to NO_PRICE, hours after it resumes until a price is defined |

## Decision rule

Constraints (a candidate is feasible when all hold):

1. **`attack_share_min`** — every median needs ≥ `attack_share_min` (34 %) of hash (analytic), and
   a coalition of that share moves pMint up or pClaim down by half its bias in ≤ 5 % of attack
   blocks (simulated; `ATTACK_MOVED_TOL`, D-WP7a-2).
2. **`max_no_price_hours`** — background NO_PRICE ≤ 6 h/yr.
3. **`halt_recall_floor`** — HALT-3 fires within a day on ≥ 90 % of `crash-70-1d` paths at the
   current `divergenceBps` (G7 coupling, D-WP7a-4).

Objective: **J = CVaR95(pClaim 90 % crash lag) / current + λ · pump overpricing / current**
(λ = `pump_overpricing_lambda`, 0.5), both terms normalised by the current windows (D-WP7a-1).
`decide_with_materiality` then keeps the current windows unless the best feasible set improves J
by more than `materiality` (20 %), breaks ties toward the current values, moves off the current set
whenever it violates a constraint, and reports BLOCKED when nothing is feasible.

**Environment-blocked constraints (D-RD-ORA-2).** Before that, `environment_adjust` relaxes what the
real environment makes unmeetable for *every* window set, so the rule still returns the least-harm
windows (verdict BLOCKED, value in the patch) instead of the current ones:
`attack_share_min` is dropped when one real pool sets every median alone (`attack_env_blocked`;
the note is raised even when the 34 % coalition test happens to pass — it is moot), and when no set
meets `max_no_price_hours` that constraint becomes "within 5 % (`NO_PRICE_BAND`) of the lowest
NO_PRICE any set reaches" — availability first, then J inside the band with the minimal-change rule. Synthetic-only
evidence makes the verdict PROVISIONAL (`final_verdict`). The **binding** text names the
constraints violated by sets that score better than the recommendation, materiality for a KEEP,
and a search bound if the choice sits on one.

- Pool feed outages: 1 per pool per 30 days, mean 4 h. This sets the background NO_PRICE: one
  pool (13.3 % of hash) out for a long spell drops the tag rate from 80 % to 66.7 % — exactly the
  ⌈2W/3⌉ fill of pMid/pSlow — so a short pMid window flickers to NO_PRICE. Replace with measured
  pool behaviour when available (pool-share CSV, tag scraping).
- `ATTACK_MOVED_TOL` = 5 % (no policy key yet; proposed `attack_moved_tol`).
- The 6-hour all-feeds outage is reported per event, not annualised (no frequency in policy).

## Findings worth knowing

- **V16 is nearly window-independent.** A coalition needs ≈ ½ of the *tagging* share (≈ 40 % of
  hash at 80 % tagging) to control any median; window length changes how long it takes, not the
  threshold.
- **A minority still shifts long medians.** Coalition quotes push the lower median up the honest
  quotes' rank order; in a long window the honest quotes span real price movement, so a 34 %
  coalition moves the slow median by ≥ 5 % in ~1 % of attack blocks.
- **pClaim's crash lag is set by the slow window alone** (pClaim = max), ≈ 21 h to 90 % of a
  one-day 70 % crash at 2,016 blocks.
- **Outage recovery scales with the window.** After a 6-hour outage a ⌈2W/3⌉ median stays
  undefined until the gap leaves the window: ≈ 16 h at the shipped windows, ≈ 26 h with pMid =
  1,152. The slow window at 2,016 rides a 6-hour outage out (it needs a > 7-hour gap).

## How real data changes the answer

With `env.data["price"]` real (hourly CSV via `ybcal data fetch` / `import`), every scenario's base
becomes a block bootstrap of the real returns (program kept), metrics are tagged `real-data`, and
the verdicts lose PROVISIONAL. Real volatility changes pump overpricing (dump speed relative to
pFast) and the attack rank-shift; real pool tagging data (outage frequency and length) is what the
NO_PRICE constraint really rests on, so it matters more than the price data here.

- **No window set meets 6 h/yr** unless the unidentified 21 % key also tags; the two-thirds fill
  (L9) against a ~72 % tagging share is what binds, and the daily swings of the real shares (the 52 %
  pool at 39–62 % per day, zpool 0–29 %) push the tag share under ⌈2W/3⌉ for hours at a time.
  A longer pMid helps in every adoption case (it averages the swings); pMid 1,152 is the least-harm
  value of the registry range.
- **No window stops the 52 % pool.** It holds 72 % of the quotes and sets every median with
  P ≈ 1 per block. Windows only delay it: with +10 % quotes pFast moves in ~1.4 h, pMid in 8.6 h
  (576) / 17 h (1,152), pSlow in ~35 h (2,016) / ~58 h (4,032); then pMint stays ≥ 5 % high on
  ~93 % of the attack's blocks (down: pClaim ≥ 5 % low on ~92 %). HALT-3 fires only during the
  capture transition (~4 % of a −30 % attack's blocks): once every median is captured they agree.
  What bounds the harm is the attestation layer once ARMED (state.cpp:366-379, math.h:286-296:
  pMint = min(xMint, aMint), pClaim = max(xClaim, aClaim), MINT-10 against pFast) — over-minting
  and premature claims under path (a) stop; the residual exposure is path (b) (pEmerg = min(xClaim,
  aClaim): emergency claims of vaults the pool's low quotes push under `emergencyRatioBps` after
  `emergencyPersist`) and griefing (HALT-3 during capture, MINT-10 refusals, the σ multiplier).
- **Withholding is the worst case and is window-independent.** If the 52 % pool keeps mining but
  stops quoting, the price is undefined within ~0.6 h and stays so (HALT-1: no mints; RED-4 needs
  pClaim, so no claims either); after it resumes the medians need ~38 h (pSlow 2,016) to refill
  (21 h at 1,152, 75 h at 4,032). The same happens for its feed outages when only ~72 % tags.
