# G5 — activation and enforcement (`signalWindow`, thresholds, floors, `activationDelay`, `valveBlocks`)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g5_activation.py` (WP-7c). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.5](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g5_activation.py`.

## What the parameters control

ACT-1 counts the signal bits of the last `signalWindow` (W) blocks. ACT-2 locks in at the first
eligible height with `count ≥ activationThreshold`; ACT-3 activates `activationDelay` blocks later.
ACT-4 sets PARTICIPATION (minting halts) when `count < participationFloor` and holds it while
`count < activationThreshold`. ACT-6 sets ENFORCEMENT (block rejection suspended) when
`count < enforcementFloor` and holds it while `count < enforcementResume`. ACT-7, the work valve
(`valveBlocks`, **node-local / excluded**), switches enforcement off for the session when a branch
rooted at a block this node rejected carries `valveBlocks` of work above its tip. §1.4 requires
`enforcementFloor < participationFloor ≤ enforcementResume < activationThreshold ≤ signalWindow`.

## Method: exact math given the share

Each block is mined by an enforcing miner with probability equal to the enforcing hash share `p`,
independently (PoW). At a constant share the count is **exactly** `Binomial(W, p)`; every sweep
metric is exact given `p` (`ybcal.sim.activation` helpers, vectorised here):

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `fh.part_hours`, `fh.enf_hours`, `fh.hours` | expected halted hours/year: episode rate × mean episode length (Little), the rate being the exact downcrossing rate of the floor `P(Bin(W−1,p) = F−1)·p(1−p)` and the length the fluid refill time `W(R−F+1)/(Wp−F+1)` (a whole year when `Wp < R`), capped by the exact bracket `P(count < R)`; conservative near the resume level (tests) |
| `fh.hours_q` | `fh.hours` at 0.01-h resolution (the primary: below it, "improvements" of 10⁻²⁰ h are noise) |
| `flaps` | expected downcrossings of both floors per year (≥ halt/clear cycles) |
| `detect.enf_p95`, `detect.part_p95` | exact **upper bound** on the p95 detection delay of a drop `p → detection_drop_share`: smallest `t` with `P(Bin(W−t,p) + Bin(t,p′) < F) ≥ 0.95` (first passage ≤ any time the count is below) |
| `act.p_lockin`, `act.blocks` | P(lock-in at the first eligible height) and `W − 1 + activationDelay` |
| `valve.minority_trip` | gambler's ruin `(q/(1−q))^(valveBlocks−1)`, `q = 1 − p`: a non-enforcing minority carries a rejected branch `valveBlocks` ahead |
| `valve.natural_trips_per_year` | `N · orphan_rate^valveBlocks` |
| `abandon.false_prob` | `false_abandon_probability` (below), at the current `abandonBlocks` |

**Share drift.** The share is not constant. Without a pool-share log the metrics are averaged over
100 equally weighted *window-mean* shares (quantiles): the default `PoolModel` pools scaled to
`expected_enforcing_share`, drifting with `HashrateDrift` (OU on share logits). **With a pool-share
log the binomial model is replaced by the real per-block replay** (next section, wave 2).

**Provenance.** Without a pool-share log every G5 result is exact math under an *assumed* share:
tagged `judgement` (not synthetic, so not PROVISIONAL); with one, `real-data`.

## Decision rules (one family each; `decide_with_materiality`, KEEP ties)

| Param | Rule |
|---|---|
| `signalWindow` | thresholds kept at their fractions of W (EF stays ≥ W/2): minimise `fh.hours_q` subject to `false_halt ≤ max_false_halt_hours_per_year`, `flaps ≤ max_flaps_per_year`, both detection bounds ≤ `max_detection_blocks`, `majority` (EF ≥ W/2), `activation_reach` |
| `participationFloor` | minimise `fh.hours_q` s.t. false-halt, flaps, `detect_part`, §1.4 ordering |
| `enforcementFloor`, `enforcementResume` | minimise `fh.hours_q` s.t. false-halt, flaps, `detect_enf`, `majority` |
| `activationThreshold` | with a pool-share log: minimise `act.spurious_lock` (the bar a coalition must clear) s.t. `act.p_reach ≥ activation_reliability`, false-halt, flaps; without one: minimise `fh.hours_q` (it is ACT-4's resume level) s.t. the same (D-RD-ACT-4) |
| `activationDelay` | minimise `act.blocks` s.t. `activationDelay ≥ operator_upgrade_window_blocks` |
| `valveBlocks` (excluded) | minimise the split length `valveBlocks` s.t. natural trips ≤ `max_valve_false_trips_per_year`, minority trip ≤ `valve_minority_trip_max` (per race; with `valve_attack_days` and a pool log, within that many days of a sustained race attack) and `valve.capstuck ≤ 0.05`; if nothing qualifies, the least-harm value (BLOCKED); verdict is a **patch-release** change |

Why false halts, not detection, are the objective: the valve bounds the consequence of minority
enforcement (spec: "the node rejoins within six blocks"), while a false halt stops minting for
everyone. Detection is a hard constraint. When `signalWindow` changes, `adjust_changes` carries the
thresholds at their fractions so the recommended set stays coherent.

**Simulation confirmation** (in `decide`, at the current and the recommended set, exact ACT-1..6 via
`activation.simulate`): activation time and false-halt hours at the expected share; a probe share
one sd above the enforcement floor (simulated halted fraction inside the analytic bracket, episodes
under the downcrossing bound); Monte-Carlo detection p95 ≤ the analytic bound (+1 %); the
`hashrate-drop-45` scenario's detection and recovery. Results sit in
`Recommendation.metrics["simulation"]`.

## Reusable: `false_abandon_probability(params, share, abandon_blocks=None)` (for G4)

Upper bound on P(ENFORCEMENT is set for `abandonBlocks` consecutive blocks within a year) without a
real abandonment: an episode starts only at a downcrossing of the floor, and lasting `A` blocks
implies the count is below `enforcementResume` at `k = ⌊(A−1)/W⌋` heights `W` apart, whose windows
are disjoint and independent of the start. So
`E[#] ≤ N·mean_p[downcrossing(p)·P(Bin(W,p) < ER)^k]`, returned capped at 1. `share` may be an
array of share samples (`share_samples(env, W)[0]`). Checked against Monte Carlo in the tests.

## Assumptions (judgement constants, `g5_activation.JUDGEMENT`)

`activation_reliability` 0.99; `valve_minority_trip_max` 0.01 per incident;
`false_halt_resolution_hours` 0.01; `detection_quantile` 0.95. The default pool split
(25/20/15/10/6/4 %, all enforcing) and drift (σ 0.05/√day) are WP-2 placeholders.

## How real data changes the answer

A pool-share CSV replaces the drift model with observed window shares (provenance `real-data`).
If the enforcing share sits closer to the floors than 0.80 (e.g. 0.64), false halts and flaps
violate the policy: the families then move (or report BLOCKED when no single parameter can fix it)
and the valve lengthens (a larger non-enforcing minority). At the default policy every current
value passes with orders of magnitude to spare and nothing is materially better: all KEEP.

## Results at `--budget quick` (seed 20261003, default policy)

All seven KEEP (`judgement`): false halts ≈ 10⁻²¹ h/yr, flaps ≈ 10⁻²⁵/yr, detection p95 of a drop to
45 % ≤ 1,833 blocks (simulated 1,835; scenario 1,788), activation 4,031 blocks; the valve's minority
trip is 9.8·10⁻⁴ at 6 blocks (5 would also pass, 16.7 % < materiality). Runtime ≈ 5 s on 4 cores.

## Real pool landscape (wave 2, D-RD-ACT-1..9)

With a pool-share log (`--data pool-shares.csv`) the study no longer assumes independent blocks at
one share. Code: `src/ybcal/sim/landscape.py`; table: `ybcal data landscape` (below).

- **The coalition is named.** `enforcing_pools` (payout-key prefixes) lists the operators expected to
  enforce; the real-data policy names ninjaraider, mining-dutch and dapool (71.9 % of blocks, the
  data agent's `expected_enforcing_share` 0.70). Without the key: the largest operators in order until
  their share reaches `expected_enforcing_share`. The old rule ("the largest pools whose cumulative
  share is closest") picked ninjaraider + an unidentified 21 % key that left the chain at block
  ≈ 3,043,700 (its hash moved to zpool the same day), which is where the rd2 report's 1,001 false-halt
  hours a year came from (D-RD-ACT-1).
- **Per-block replay.** The coalition's real block indicator is bootstrapped in 3-day stretches
  (circular moving blocks: longer than a signal window, so the within-window structure is real) to
  1 year (quick) / 2 years (standard) × 64 paths, common to every candidate; the exact ACT-4/ACT-6
  hysteresis (`state.cpp:1242-1249`) runs on it with the chain ACTIVE. `fh.*` and `flaps` are those
  halts (every one is false: the members mine honestly; only their share moves). ACT-2 lock-in
  (`state.cpp:1084`) is measured from a random start: `act.p_reach` = P(lock-in within
  `activation_reach_days`; 0 = the first eligible height) (D-RD-ACT-2, -3).
- **Spurious lock-in** (`act.spurious_lock`): the largest P(lock-in within the reach) among the
  coalitions of the six largest operators whose mean share is 40–60 % — they cannot hold the floors
  once ACTIVE. ACT-2 is a single-window test, so a multi-day hop of an auto-switching pool can lock in
  such a coalition. It is the activation family's objective (the threshold is the bar a coalition
  must clear) and a constraint only if `max_spurious_lock_prob` < 1 (D-RD-ACT-4).
- **The work valve under a sustained race attack** (`valve.attack_trip`, with `valve_attack_days`):
  a rule-breaking transaction (a matured vault swept without burning YED) sits in every stock
  mempool, so each stock block on the enforcers' tip starts a race and a lost race hands the
  transaction to the next one; the real non-enforcing block sequence is replayed for the attack
  horizon and the first trip recorded (`landscape.valve_race_trips`). `valve.capstuck` is the exact
  probability that a genuine-minority enforcer (stock share `1 − detection_drop_share`) ends at the
  64-note cap (`index.h:65`, `index.cpp:607`) instead of tripping and rejoining (DP over lead ×
  notes). The valve family keeps `valve.capstuck ≤ 0.05`; when no `valveBlocks` meets the attack
  bound it reports the least-harm value (smallest attack trip that keeps the cap bound), BLOCKED
  (D-RD-ACT-5).
- **The window family moves `signalWindow` only** (thresholds at their fractions; rows that move a
  threshold alone belong to the threshold families), and its grid is centred on the current
  window. When the new window satisfies every budget, the threshold families' own changes are
  dropped and each threshold keeps its fraction (minimal change; the L3 fractions 75/60/50/60 % are
  what the owner fixed, D-RD-ACT-2, -9).
- **Scenario confirmation** runs every scenario tagged `activation` (the `hashrate-drop` family and
  the new `real-pools` family) through ACT-1..6 at the current and recommended sets: detection,
  recovery, W19 window, abandonment, the blocks with enforcement on while the true enforcing share
  is below one half (ACT-7 exposure) and the valve's trip probability under attack (D-RD-ACT-6).
