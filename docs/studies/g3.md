# G3 — collateral and classes (`baseRatioBps[3]`, `classMin/Max[3]`, `claimThresholdBps`, `emergencyRatioBps`)

Code: `src/ybcal/studies/g3_collateral.py` (WP-7b). Plan: [PLAN.md §5.3](../PLAN.md).
Tests: `tests/studies/test_g3_collateral.py`. Decisions: D-WP7b-1 … D-WP7b-3, D-WP7b-7 in
[decisions.md](../decisions.md).

## What the parameters control

- **`baseRatioBps[c]`** — MINT-5: a class-`c` mint must lock `debt × baseRatio × σ-multiplier / 10⁸`
  worth of YEC at pMint (wallet rounding to 1,000 zat, at least `4·feeMin`). MINT-4/MINT-6 also read
  it (W16 recap exemption, W20 soft cap).
- **`classMin/Max[c]`** — MINT-2: the lock length (blocks) of a class; contiguous
  (`classMin[c+1] = classMax[c] + 1`). A: 30–90 days, B: 90 days–1 year, C: 1–5 years.
- **`claimThresholdBps`** (θ) — RED-4(a): a matured vault is claimable when
  `collateral · pClaim < debt · θ`; RED-5 caps the claimant at `debt · θ / pClaim`, which under (a) is
  always ≥ the collateral (residual 0, design note G3-DN2).
- **`emergencyRatioBps`** (e) — RED-4(b) / NOT-1 (ARMED only): the same test at
  `pEmerg = min(xClaim, aClaim)` and ratio `e`, after a notice persisted `emergencyPersist` blocks; the
  claimant gets the debt's worth at pClaim, the owner the residual.

**Fact 1.5-1 drives the study**: the vault script has no spend but the owner's before
`lockHeight + grace`, so collateral must cover the debt for the whole term plus grace.

## Method

1. **Hour ensemble** (`ensemble()`; shared with G4). Synthetic: the four WP-2 presets `gbm`, `merton`,
   `garch`, `regime` (≈ 120 % annualised volatility, `$0.40` start), `budget.paths // 4` paths each
   (quick 16, standard 100, deep 500) over `hour_horizon_years` (quick 5.2, else 6). Real data
   (`env.data["price"]` a real `PricePath`): `bootstrap` (stationary block bootstrap of the real hourly
   returns, one-week mean block, started at the last real price) and `history` (the real path as one
   path, so every real start date is used). Each member goes through WP-3's hour-mode oracle kernel
   (`OracleTransferKernel.ideal`, 4 sub-steps — WP-4's default, within 0.1 pp of 12 sub-steps) to get
   hourly pMint, pClaim, σ multiplier and the NO_PRICE/DIVERGENCE bits. Built once per process
   (memoised on seed, budget, data hash and the G1/G2 parameters it reads); every candidate shares it
   (common random numbers).
   Every member's log returns are shifted to the policy's drift convention `price_drift`
   (`ybcal.sim.drift`; default centred = expected log drift 0, D-RD-AUD-1); the real `history`
   member keeps its realised drift.
2. **P(bad debt)** = WP-4 `metrics.p_bad_debt_fast` per member and class (memoised on what it reads:
   the class's ratio and bounds, grace, feeMin, σ choice, term grid). Start hours every
   `start_stride_hours` (quick 24, standard 6, deep 2) after the σ / slow-median warm-up; terms = the
   equal-probability grid of `term_distribution` (quick 8, else 16 points); σ multiplier at
   `sigma_mult_at` (default the path's median). Test asserts identity with a direct WP-4 call.
3. **Aggregation** over members with `optimize.robust.aggregate(·, ensemble_agg)` — default
   `"worst"` (minimax over the presets; a benefit takes the worst = smallest). Per-member values are
   kept in the table and the evidence.
4. **Claim study** (θ; independent of the ratios, memoised once for the whole θ lattice): on every
   member, claim-open hours every week after warm-up; at each, matured vaults whose owner is away with
   coverage `c0 × θ` at the true price, `c0 ∈ {1.05, 1.15, 1.3, 1.6}`. Trigger = first hour within a
   year with `collateral · pClaim < debt · θ` (exact integer level, `FirstBelow` sparse table). At the
   trigger the claimant receives the whole collateral (RED-5 under (a)), pays FEE-1 and the 1,000-zat
   network fee, sells at the true price less `claimant_slippage_bps` (+ depth impact when a depth CSV
   is loaded, at the `100 − claimant_slippage_pctl` percentile of depth) and buys the debt in YED at
   `1 + yed_premium_bps`.
5. **Emergency study** (e): `crash-70-1d` and `crash-90-30d` at hour resolution through the same
   kernel; matured, owner-away vaults with coverage `c0 × θ` (`c0 ∈ {1.02, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5}`) at hour 48.
   RED-4(a) closes at the first hour pClaim shows them underwater **and** the claimant's margin clears
   `claimant_min_profit_bps`; RED-4(b) (ARMED, `aClaim` = the true price) at the first hour
   `min(pClaim, true)` has shown them underwater at `e` for `ceil(emergencyPersist/48)` + 1 consecutive
   hours, executed by a YED holder exiting at par (D-WP4-6: never profitable to a claimant at par).
6. **Class boundaries**: shifted partitions (quick: A|B at 60/120 days, B|C at 270/548 days; standard:
   six and five positions) are scored as evidence.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| `pbad.{A,B,C}` (+ `.member`) | P(collateral at the true price < debt at the first block above `lockHeight + grace`), term-grid mean, aggregate over members |
| `pbad_lock.{c}` | the same at the first block above `lockHeight` |
| `es.{c}` | E[max(0, 1 − value/debt)] at the claim opening: severity (D-RD-AUD-3) |
| `tmax_ok_days.{c}` | longest grid term whose P(bad debt) meets the class tolerance (term reach at this ratio) |
| `emerg.shortfall_stress`, `emerg.b_share_stress` | the emergency metrics with YED at `emergency_stress_premium_bps` (−2,000): RED-4(b) closes only when the exit pays (D-RD-AUD-4) |
| `het.{c}` | (mean P(bad) over the longest quarter of the term grid − mean over the shortest quarter) / class mean (0 if the mean is 0), aggregate (worst) |
| `yed_per_usd.{c}` | 10⁸ / (ratio × median σ multiplier): YED minted per USD of collateral |
| `sigma_med.{c}` | median hour-mode σ multiplier after warm-up, median over members |
| `claim.mean_bps`, `p10_bps`, `p50_bps` | claimant net margin at the first RED-4(a) trigger, bps of debt (worst member) |
| `claim.show_up` | share of triggers with margin ≥ `claimant_min_profit_bps` |
| `claim.bad` | share already bad debt (collateral < debt) at the trigger |
| `claim.forfeit_bps` | mean collateral value above the debt at the trigger: what an absent owner loses (RED-5 residual 0) |
| `emerg.shortfall` / `emerg.shortfall_a` | mean `max(0, 1 − collateral value / debt)` at closure with RED-4(b) available / with (a) only (at the horizon end if never closed), worst crash scenario |
| `emerg.benefit`, `emerg.b_share`, `emerg.b_par_loss_bps` | shortfall reduction; share closed by (b); the par-exit holder's loss `1 − true/pClaim` |

## Decision rules

| Param | Rule |
|---|---|
| `baseRatioBps[c]` | The smallest ratio on the 2,500-bps lattice inside the registry bounds with aggregate `pbad.c ≤ max_bad_debt_prob[c]` (σ at `sigma_mult_at`). `decide_with_materiality` with primary = the ratio: a feasible current value is KEEP unless the smallest feasible ratio frees more than `materiality` of the collateral; a violating current value moves to the smallest feasible ratio. **No feasible ratio → BLOCKED** and the recommended value is the *least-violating* one (lowest aggregate P(bad debt), ties toward current), with the whole trade-off curve in `metrics["tradeoff"]` and `g3_tradeoff.csv`. |
| `claimThresholdBps` | The smallest θ (250-bps lattice) with `claim.mean_bps ≥ claimant_min_profit_bps` ("E[claim profit] > fee + min profit at the policy slippage"); materiality on θ; BLOCKED (highest margin) if none. |
| `emergencyRatioBps` | e on the 100-bps lattice strictly between 10,000 and the current θ minimising `emerg.shortfall`; materiality. The metric is monotone in e (an earlier (b) closure always covers more), so the rule favours e just under θ; premature-claim risk from noisy attested prices is G8's `emergencyPersist` question and is not modelled here. |
| `classMax[0]`, `classMin[1]`, `classMax[1]`, `classMin[2]` | Verified (D-WP7b-3): KEEP while contiguity and the CLTV bound hold. Heterogeneity above `class_heterogeneity_max` triggers PLAN §5.3's "split or merge" suggestion, which with `NUM_CLASSES = 3` is a rule change → design note G3-DN6; shifted partitions are reported as evidence (they only move the problem). |
| `classMin[0]`, `classMax[2]` | Product choices (30 days = grace, 5 years): verified, KEEP. |

Every verdict passes `final_verdict`: synthetic-only evidence → PROVISIONAL; BLOCKED stays BLOCKED.

## Design notes (`design_notes(results)` and `metrics["design_notes"]`)

`g3_collateral.design_notes(results, policy)` returns a list of dicts
`{id, title, finding, evidence, consequence, fix, params}` computed from the current row; each
Recommendation carries the notes whose `params` include it in `metrics["design_notes"]` (the report
deduplicates by `id`). G3 raises: **DN1** no liquidation before `lock + grace`; **DN2** RED-5 residual
always 0 under RED-4(a); **DN3** pClaim lag eats the claim margin; **DN4** RED-4(b) pays at pClaim (a
loss at par); **DN5** the σ multiplier sits at 1× at its median; **DN6** (when triggered) term
heterogeneity inside the classes. G4 and G9 use the same format (`G4-DN*`, `G9-DN*`).

## Assumptions

- Hour mode with the *ideal* kernel (no oracle noise or bias): the claim study's pClaim lag is the
  rolling-median lag only. A calibrated kernel (`calibrate_kernel`) would add pool noise.
- The refHeight choice (40 blocks) collapses to one hourly snapshot (WP-4 D-WP4-4).
- Vaults are $1,000; FEE-1 is proportional above the floor, so P(bad debt) is size-free except for
  the `4·feeMin` floor (never binding at these prices).
- Claim and emergency populations are owner-away vaults at a coverage grid relative to θ (ratio-free
  by construction, so a ratio sweep does not recompute them).
- `"worst"` aggregation makes the strictest preset (usually `regime`) decide; set `ensemble_agg` to
  `"mean"` or `"cvar"` in the policy to change that.

## How real data changes the answer

With a real hourly price in `env.data["price"]` the ensemble becomes the block bootstrap plus the
history itself, provenance becomes `real-data` (verdicts lose PROVISIONAL), and the ratios move with
the real drawdown distribution over 30-day to 5-year horizons. A depth CSV adds liquidation impact to
the claimant slippage. The crash scenarios stay synthetic programs (the emergency rule remains
PROVISIONAL until a real-data stress set is added).

## Budgets

quick (16 paths × 4 presets × 5.2 years, 24-h stride, 8 terms): ≈ 50 s serial, ≈ 100 s through
`run_group(workers=4)` (each worker builds the ensemble). standard (100 paths, 6-h stride, 16 terms):
≈ 10–15 min. deep (500 paths, 2-h stride): overnight.
