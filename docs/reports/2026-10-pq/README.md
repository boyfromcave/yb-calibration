# Post-quantum growth and the `-pqfeerate` multiplier — October 2026

Ycash Yellowback (YED), post-quantum line (`upgrade/quantum-vaults`), quantum plan Q7 and §9 item 3.
Generated by `ybcal pq --out docs/reports/2026-10-pq` (study: [docs/studies/pq.md](../../studies/pq.md));
the full tables are in [pq.md](pq.md), the numbers in [pq.json](pq.json). Analytic: no price data, no
devnet, runs in under a second. Byte layouts from `yellowback-quantum-spec.md` (frozen 2026-10-10);
node constants from ycash-dd `ec9abe852` and ycash6 `0f150defb` (`upgrade/quantum-vaults`).

## 1. Recommendation

**Ship `-pqfeerate` at m = 50 × the relay floor, i.e. 5,000 zat/KB, on both node lines, with ycash6's
size term as a third `max` argument (`max(-yellowbackfee, ZIP-317, rate × size)`), not added.**

- **P2PKH parity needs m ≥ 40.8.** At 1× a PQ byte pays 0.1 zat; an ordinary 245-byte P2PKH payment
  pays 4.08 zat a byte. ycash-dd's block template fills half the block by priority, then by fee rate, so
  1× PQ spends would be placed after every ordinary payment.
- **Cross-line identity needs m ≥ 33.6.** ycash6's ZIP-317 conventional fee already charges 500 zat per
  150 bytes of transparent input (3,333 zat/KB, about 33× the floor). Below 33.6 the two lines charge
  different fees for the same PQ transaction: at 1× an SLH redeem costs 1,000 zat on ycash-dd and
  27,500 zat on ycash6. ycash6 miners also put a transaction that pays less than the conventional fee
  in their lower bucket (`miner.cpp:644-648`), so a ycash-dd wallet at 1× would be deprioritised by
  ycash6 miners on the same network. From 34 on, the size term binds on both lines and the fees match
  to the zat.
- **Sapling parity needs only m ≥ 4.2.** A Sapling z→z pays 0.42 zat a byte.
- **The cost to users is negligible.** At m = 50 the dearest path (an SLH-holder redeem, 16 KB) costs
  80,410 zat, which is $0.08 at the policy's worst price of $100/YEC and $0.0003 at $0.40. A Falcon
  transfer costs 9,725 zat, and an SLH owner redeem with P2PKH holders 41,245 zat.
- 50 is the first round candidate above 40.8 and leaves a margin for larger owner spends (`n_yed` > 1)
  and future ordinary-fee drift.

**What the multiplier does not do.** It sets what honest wallets pay. It does not set what a block
stuffer pays. Relay accepts any transaction paying `min(100 zat/KB × size, 1,000)`, so a 2 MB block
of any kind of transaction (PQ, P2PKH or Sapling) costs a spammer about 0.002 YEC (124,000–200,000 zat).
That is the same today and is unchanged by this plan (D-Q-7 keeps relay out of the change).
`-pqrelaybytes` is the operator's lever for PQ-specific block-space caps.

Mints and redeems come from `policy/default.toml` `adoption_scenarios`. Transfers, claims and bridge

transfers dominate. The stress row reproduces the plan §6 estimate (10,000 SLH spends a day ≈ 30 GB a
year).

## 4. Validation cost (structural; benchmarks pending)

- A full block holds 248 SLH-DSA spends or 1,188 Falcon spends. The policy sigop cost caps a template
  at 1,000 `OP_CHECKPQSIG`. Consensus counts 0 (spec F-5), so a miner's own block can hold 1,188.
- The comparison is a block of 13,513 P2PKH inputs, one ECDSA verify each. A PQ-filled block verifies
  no slower than that while `t_SLH ≤ 54.5 × t_ECDSA` and `t_Falcon ≤ 11.4 × t_ECDSA`.
- **Placeholders:** `VERIFY_US` is unset. `q/pqlib` (ycash-dd `d4038d544`, WIP) has committed no
  benchmark output yet. Fill it from the Q1 benchmark table to get seconds per block.

## 5. Other effects

- **G9 dust.** At m = 50, `minOutput`'s lower bound (3 × the cost of spending a token at $100/YEC)
  rises from $0.003 to $0.029 with Falcon holders and to $0.12 with SLH holders. Both stay below the
  shipped values, so no G9 change follows.
- **`VAULT_RPC_FEE` = 10,000 zat** (flat) is below the m = 50 fee of an SLH owner spend (about 40,000).
  The vault RPCs should take the same `max(VAULT_RPC_FEE, rate × size)`. Today they would underpay
  relative to the Yellowback wallet path.
