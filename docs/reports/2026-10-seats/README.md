# Attestor seats under D-Q-29..D-Q-31 — October 2026

Ycash Yellowback (YED), post-quantum line (`upgrade/quantum-vaults`), the owner's 2026-10-11 attestor rules.
Generated by `ybcal seats --out docs/reports/2026-10-seats` (study: [docs/studies/seats.md](../../studies/seats.md));
the tables are in [seats.md](seats.md), the numbers in [seats.json](seats.json). Exact integer evaluation of
the node's rules as built on ycash-dd `q/attest` `4bb710b4c` (bit for bit with its Python model); no price
pulls, no sweeps, a few seconds. Dollars at the $0.40 reference of the frozen 2026-10-04 data.

## Findings

1. **Seats go to the biggest bonds; tenure is worth at most ×1.9 × 1.05 ≈ ×2.** A fresh 20,000-YEC bond
   outranks a year-old seated 5,000-YEC bond at its first snapshot (20,000 × 1.413 against 5,000 × 1.9 ×
   1.05). So nine 20,000-YEC bonders fill all nine seats however many 5,000-YEC members there are, and a
   5,000-YEC bonder holds a seat only while fewer than nine members bond more than about 7,060 YEC
   (at a year's tenure, ×1.41 for a set with room) or 9,975 YEC (to evict from a full set).
2. **Waiting never takes a seat.** An equal bond, or 1.05×, never displaces a seated member (strict
   hysteresis); 1.06× takes about 324 days against a year-old seat, 1.1× about 207, 1.25× 49, and from
   1.5× a newcomer is seated the moment it is eligible (14 days).
3. **Eviction is cheap against floor-sized bonds.** Any bond ≥ 5,000 YEC evicts a member that is not
   live. A live 5,000-YEC member is evicted by 5,250 YEC on its first day and by 9,975 YEC at a year;
   a 20,000-YEC member by 21,000 to 39,900 YEC. Evicted members are not slashed; their bonds return 14
   days later.
4. **Capture capital falls with the floor.** Taking 7 of 9 seats (D-RD-ATT-4's harmful capture) from a
   year-old honest set of 5,000-YEC bonds costs 69,825 YEC ($27,930) into a full set and 49,419 YEC with
   room; from 20,000-YEC bonds, 279,299 / 197,678 YEC. v3's flat 20,000-YEC minimum put the floor at
   140,000 YEC. In the trace an entrant joining one 9,975-YEC bond a day replaces all fifteen
   year-old 5,000-YEC members in 15 days and holds 7 seats on day 21 (all nine by day 23); at 5,250 YEC
   it cannot enter at all (the youngest honest member, 30 days old, needs 7,999 YEC to evict).
5. **The bond minimum sits at the floor for a long time.** clamp(5 % of YED vault collateral ÷ 9, 5,000,
   20,000) binds at 5,000 YEC up to 900,000 YEC of collateral ($120,000 of YED at 300 % and $0.40) and
   at 20,000 YEC from 3.6 M YEC. It gates entry only: once a set is contested the seated bonds are set
   by the ranking, not by the minimum.

**Reading for the owner.** D-Q-29/30 make the seat price a market: honest operators who want to keep
their seats must bond near what an attacker would, and the tenure bonus caps at about twice the bond.
At launch, with the minimum at its floor, the cost of harmful capture is set by what the honest
attestors choose to bond, not by the rule. If the owner wants the old 140,000-YEC capture floor back,
the lever is the floor (`bondMinFloorZat`), not the share; at 20,000 YEC the capture cost against a
full year-old set is 279,299 YEC.
