# Attestor seats under D-Q-29..D-Q-31 — October 2026

Ycash Yellowback (YED), post-quantum line (`upgrade/quantum-vaults`), the owner's 2026-10-11 attestor rules
with the bond floor at 10,000 YEC (D-Q-30 as amended by the owner on 2026-10-11, after the first run of this
study; it was 5,000 YEC). Generated by `ybcal seats --out docs/reports/2026-10-seats` (study:
[docs/studies/seats.md](../../studies/seats.md)); the tables are in [seats.md](seats.md), the numbers in
[seats.json](seats.json). Exact integer evaluation of the node's rules as built on ycash-dd `q/attest`
`f07a9aef6` (bit for bit with its Python model); no price pulls, no sweeps, a few seconds. Dollars at the
$0.40 reference of the frozen 2026-10-04 data.

## Findings

1. **Seats go to the biggest bonds; tenure is worth at most ×1.9 × 1.05 ≈ ×2.** A fresh 20,000-YEC bond
   outranks a year-old seated 10,000-YEC (floor) bond at its first snapshot (20,000 × 1.413 = 28,260
   against 10,000 × 1.9 × 1.05 = 19,950). So nine 20,000-YEC bonders fill all nine seats however many
   floor-sized members there are, and a floor-sized bonder holds a seat only while fewer than nine members
   bond more than about 14,120 YEC (at a year's tenure, ×1.41 for a set with room) or 19,950 YEC (to evict
   from a full set).
2. **Waiting never takes a seat.** An equal bond, or 1.05×, never displaces a seated member (strict
   hysteresis); 1.06× takes about 324 days against a year-old seat, 1.1× about 207, 1.25× 49, and from
   1.5× a newcomer is seated the moment it is eligible (14 days). (Ratios, unchanged by the floor.)
3. **Eviction against floor-sized bonds.** Any bond ≥ 10,000 YEC evicts a member that is not live. A live
   10,000-YEC member is evicted by 10,500 YEC on its first day and by 19,950 YEC at a year; a 20,000-YEC
   member by 21,000 to 39,900 YEC. Evicted members are not slashed; their bonds return 14 days later.
4. **The 10,000-YEC floor restores v3's capture cost.** Taking 7 of 9 seats (D-RD-ATT-4's harmful
   capture) from a year-old honest set of floor-sized bonds costs 139,650 YEC ($55,860) into a full set
   and 98,839 YEC with room (all nine: 179,549 YEC); at the old 5,000-YEC floor it was 69,825 / 49,419.
   From 20,000-YEC bonds, 279,299 / 197,678 YEC. v3's flat 20,000-YEC minimum put the floor at 140,000
   YEC. In the trace an entrant joining one 19,950-YEC bond a day replaces all fifteen floor-sized members
   (nine a year old, six 30 days) in 15 days and holds 7 seats on day 21 (all nine by day 23); at 10,500
   YEC it cannot enter at all (the youngest honest member, 30 days old, needs 15,998 YEC to evict).
   *Caveat (W4-K-3):* the capture cost above prices taking 7 of 9 seats; one membership of the open set (a 10,000-YEC bond, returned 14 days after exit) can cancel a vault's first claim (CAN-2 needs 2, 3, then 4 co-signers for the next ones), and that cheaper cancel cost is not modelled here. The floor is not calibrated against the claim/cancel mechanics (see `docs/decisions.md` D-RD-COL-6/7 amendment).
5. **The bond minimum sits at the floor for longer.** clamp(5 % of YED vault collateral ÷ 9, 10,000,
   20,000) binds at 10,000 YEC up to 1.8 M YEC of collateral ($240,000 of YED at 300 % and $0.40; it was
   900,000 YEC at the 5,000 floor) and at 20,000 YEC from 3.6 M YEC. Nine floor seats lock 90,000 YEC. It
   gates entry only: once a set is contested the seated bonds are set by the ranking, not by the minimum.

**Reading for the owner.** D-Q-29/30 make the seat price a market: honest operators who want to keep
their seats must bond near what an attacker would, and the tenure bonus caps at about twice the bond.
At launch, with the minimum at its floor, the cheapest honest set (nine year-old 10,000-YEC seats)
costs an attacker about 140,000 YEC for 7 seats, which is v3's figure; a set with room (fewer than
fifteen members) costs about 99,000 YEC, so the honest set should be kept full. A younger honest set is
cheaper (73,500 YEC against day-0 seats, 111,984 at 30 days).
