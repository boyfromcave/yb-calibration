**Red flag 1 — corrected to the circulating base (W4-K-2).** The table's "MINT-6 cap after 30 d /
365 d" columns are the retired issuance base (coinbase since the start height). D-Q-25's shipped
rule reads the circulating supply (16,972,537.5 YEC at height 3,062,423). On that base `maxVaultShareBps`
1,000 does not bound one vault at $0.40: the share is about $101,835, so `maxMint` $100,000 is the
ceiling, the share reaches `maxMint` near $0.393, and it exceeds the $34,675 p50 executable claim
above about $0.136. The owner ruling is absent and the node constant was not changed. Class C's base
equals `recapRatioBps`, so the aggregate cap never refuses a class C mint.

**Concentration (G3 claimant model, frozen bid books).** A claim at θ executes only if the claimant
nets 2 % of the debt after selling the seized YEC with the G3 slippage model (100 bps + 200 bps per
book sold). The largest executable debt on the 2026-10-04 books: $258 in G3's own 2 % band at p10,
$800 at p50; $5,505 / $15,298 within 10 % of the best bid; **$25,815 / $34,675 against the whole
visible bid book** ($3,709 / $4,982, three venues).

| Case (cap $1.02 M at $0.40) | vaults | debt each | claim executes (p10 / p50 whole book) | one sale vs p10 daily volume | one owner absent / key lost |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| one owner at 25 % of the cap | 1 | $254,588 | no / no | 12.7× | 100 % |
| the same over 100 vaults | 100 | $2,546 | yes / yes | 0.13× | 1 % |
| one owner at 50 % of the cap | 1 | $509,176 | no / no | 25.5× | 100 % |
| the same over 100 vaults | 100 | $5,092 | yes / yes | 0.25× | 1 % |

The price-driven part of bad debt (G3's `max_bad_debt_prob` per class) is the same for one vault and
for a hundred — all vaults share the price. What concentration changes is (a) executability: a whale
vault that goes under θ cannot be claimed at any plausible depth, so it stays under-collateralised
until the market deepens (bad debt by the G3 definition), and (b) idiosyncratic risk: the policy's
owner-absence model (one spell a year, median 7 days) and `lost_key_prob` act on 100 % of the position
instead of 1 %. **A per-vault share-of-cap limit is needed** (table above). **Red flag 2:** $100,000 is
3× the largest claim the frozen books can absorb; D-Q-24's `maxMint` is a bet on depth growing with
adoption, not a value the data supports — H-12's $2,500 was the depth-consistent figure (10 % of a
$25,000 p10 daily volume).

Does the attestor set pay its way? The policy's only attestor economics are G8's: a seat must earn
`attestor_min_monthly_revenue_usd` = $50 and cover the bond's opportunity cost (20,000 YEC × price ×
5 % / 12: $33 at $0.40, $833 at $10). With supply-consistent sizes the bands yield $7 a seat a month in

for users, and the 2026-10 real-data report already found the attestor floor unmeetable at today's
adoption under any fee it tried (§3.4 there).

## 4. Early fee on debt vs collateral: the claim path

An in-term claim (θ = 125 %) takes `θ × debt` worth of collateral at `pClaim`, so the owner's loss above
the debt is at most 25 % of the debt. Under the collateral rule the class-A early fee was 15 % of the
debt at 300 % (D-IT-16 noted it "approaches the cost of being claimed"); on the debt it is 5 %, class
B 2.5 %, class C 1 %. Early redemption now beats being claimed by 5× in A and 25× in C, so no owner is
nudged toward the claim path, and over-collateralising no longer raises the fee. Gaming paths checked:
the owner cannot choose `pMint` (attested); a claim needs the price to fall under θ, which the owner
cannot cause cheaply; the debt is fixed at mint, so the fee cannot be shrunk by moving collateral; and
the −70 % shock row (§5) shows the early fee is worth 30 % of its mint-time dollars after a crash, but a
300 % class-A vault is then at 90 % and claimable anyway, so a cheap early exit after a crash is
exactly the behaviour the system wants.

  itself stays 0.1 YEC for plain payments). Alternatively lower the targets: at $0.01/KB the cap still
  binds below $0.82 on the redeem — no target that charges more than $0.04 per redeem clears 0.1 YEC at $0.40.

## Red flags for the owner

1. **MINT-6b does not bound one vault on the circulating base** (D-Q-25): at $0.40 the 1,000 bps share
   is above `maxMint`, so one vault is $100,000, which the frozen books cannot absorb (§2). The
   $39,420 column is the retired issuance base. The owner ruling is absent; 1,000 bps is unchanged.
2. **$100,000 is unliquidatable on today's books** (largest executable claim $34,675 against the whole
   visible bid side; $800 in G3's own band). A per-vault share-of-cap limit is recommended, and
   `maxMint` $100k is a depth bet (§2).
3. **`-maxtxfee` 0.1 YEC voids the $/KB targets at today's price** on every vault / Yellowback path; a
   dollar cap for those builders is the fix (§5).
4. **The $/KB targets drop below P2PKH parity above $1,225 (yb) / $490 (pq)**; the floor must be the pq
   study's 5,000 zat/KB, not 1× relay (§5).
5. **The bands do not pay the attestor set at low adoption** with supply-consistent sizes ($7 a seat a
   month against a $33 bond cost and a $50 floor) — nor does today's rule ($3); the owner decides (§3).

## Assumptions (all stated in the code)
